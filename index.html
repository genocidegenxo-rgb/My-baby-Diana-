A Letter for Diana

Mi niña bonita,

I made this little place because I never want the world to reduce you to one thing. You are not a photograph, a first impression, or a list of pretty words. You are a whole person with a mind of your own, a history that belongs to you, a thousand little habits, hopes, opinions, and dreams.

I want to keep learning you. I want to know the stories behind your favorite things, the reasons you love certain songs, what makes you laugh when nobody else understands the joke, what you dream about when the world gets quiet, and all the little details you might think nobody notices.

This museum will never be finished, because there will always be another side of you for me to discover. And I hope you keep discovering yourself, too. I am proud of the person you are and excited for the person you are still becoming.

May this little corner remind you that your story matters, your voice matters, and you deserve to be known in all your beautiful complexity.

Always yours ♡
